including contributed code. Without an explicit grant from every contributor, a single contributed
patch would make both impossible for the file it touches.

Collecting this up front is cheap. Retrofitting it after dozens of contributors is not.

your Contribution is submitted under it, and that the representations in Section 5 are true for
