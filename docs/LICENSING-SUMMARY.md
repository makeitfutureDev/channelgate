# Licensing summary

ChannelGate is **source-available**, not OSI open-source software. The binding terms are the
[Makeitfuture Sustainable Use License 1.3](../LICENSE.md), the [EE directory license](../src/ee/LICENSE-EE.md)
and any separate agreement with the Licensor. This summary does not replace them.

- Internal business, personal and noncommercial use are permitted within the applicable limits.
- No key permits one conversation; a free key permits unlimited conversations, each with 500 AI
  messages per UTC month. Enterprise entitlements can remove those limits.
- Dedicated deployment services for individual customers are permitted when root Section 3.1's
  conditions hold. Paid software distribution, shared customer hosting and white-labeling require
  the applicable written agreement.
- Permitted free redistribution includes the **unchanged bundled EE directory**. That exception
  lets ordinary no-key and free-key installations run their required enforcement components;
  it does not unlock optional Enterprise features or permit disabling enforcement.
- **Composio SDK mode is Enterprise-only and Beta.** Standard Composio MCP mode remains available
  in every tier. Google Chat and Microsoft Teams are Beta connectors available in every tier.
- Filesystem/process isolation, authorization, secret handling, audit records and backup/restore
  retain the same protections in every tier.

for limits, and [privacy/data flow](PRIVACY-AND-DATA-FLOW.md) for exact licensing payloads.

[original terms](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). [Trademark permissions](../TRADEMARK.md) and AI/provider
